# Mentoring a Junior Colleague

| **Field** | **Content** |
| :--- | :--- | 
| **Date** | 17 Aug 2026 |
| **Activity** | Mentoring |
| **Duration** | 30 mins |
| **Objective** | Provide constructive career advice |
| **Session Focus** | After learning I would be leaving my R2 role, an R1 colleague reached out for advice. They had been asked if they were interested in being my replacement but gave a non-committal answer. They were waiting on the outcome of another slightly more advanced R1 role and also had interview fatigue after recently losing out for another open R2 position. I advised them to call our manager back, express strong interest in the R2 role, and explain that their hesitation was due to not wanting to turn down the better R1 role if offered. I explained that honesty is the best policy. It is fine to say the R2 role is their priority, while accepting the R1 role in the meantime so they do not risk ending up with neither. |
| **Reflection** | I felt I may have been too forceful and next time will be less concrete "do this" and more "this is an option". However, the colleague ended the call by thanking me for revitalising their enthusiasm and said they would take my advice. |
| **Evidence** | None, informal ad-hoc teams call |
