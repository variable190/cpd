### The "Three-Word" Test for Valid CPD
For an activity to count, it must pass this test:
1.  **Relevance:** Is it directly related to your current or future role as a penetration tester?
2.  **Intentionality:** Did you do it specifically to learn, not just passively consume?
3.  **Reflection:** Can you articulate exactly how this changed your thinking or work?

### Diversify Your Activities
Relying solely on labs is risky; auditors look for a **balance** of activities. As a triager aiming for penetration testing, include:
*   **Structured Learning:** Formal courses (e.g., CRT prep, SANS).
*   **Reflective Learning:** Reading specific RFCs, analyzing new CVEs, or reviewing post-mortem reports of major breaches.
*   **Professional Contribution:** Mentoring a junior analyst, writing an internal guide on a new attack vector, or presenting a "lunch and learn" at your company.
*   **Community Engagement:** Attending meetups (e.g., OWASP, local security groups) or participating in CTFs.
