### The "Three-Word" Test for Valid CPD
For an activity to count, it must pass this test:
1.  **Relevance:** Is it directly related to your current or future role as a penetration tester?
2.  **Intentionality:** Did you do it specifically to learn, not just passively consume?
3.  **Reflection:** Can you articulate exactly how this changed your thinking or work?

### How to Structure Your CPD Record
You should maintain a simple spreadsheet or document with the following columns for every entry (including your HTB machines):

| Field | What to Record | Example Entry (HTB Machine) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Date** | Date completed | 15 Aug 2026 |
| **Activity** | Title and Type | "Machine: 'DumpsterDivem' (Self-Directed Lab)" |
| **Duration** | Hours spent | 4.5 Hours |
| **Learning Objective** | What did you aim to learn? | "Understand the exploitation chain for CVE-2024-XXXX on IIS servers." |
| **Key Learnings** | Technical takeaways | "Learned to bypass WAF using specific header fragmentation; practiced privilege escalation via misconfigured service permissions." |
| **Reflection (Crucial)** | **How does this impact your work?** | "I previously missed this vector in triage. I will now add header fragmentation checks to my standard web testing checklist for client engagements." |
| **Evidence** | Location of proof | Link to private write-up or screenshot of root flag (do not post public spoilers). |

### Diversify Your Activities
Relying solely on labs is risky; auditors look for a **balance** of activities. As a triager aiming for penetration testing, include:
*   **Structured Learning:** Formal courses (e.g., CRT prep, SANS).
*   **Reflective Learning:** Reading specific RFCs, analyzing new CVEs, or reviewing post-mortem reports of major breaches.
*   **Professional Contribution:** Mentoring a junior analyst, writing an internal guide on a new attack vector, or presenting a "lunch and learn" at your company.
*   **Community Engagement:** Attending meetups (e.g., OWASP, local security groups) or participating in CTFs.
